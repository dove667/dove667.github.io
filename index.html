Research

Most AI assistants you have talked to — ChatGPT, Siri, Copilot — work in turns: they wait for you to finish, then answer. Full-duplex Omni models drop that constraint: a single model listens, thinks, and speaks at the same time, so it can interject, react, and stay in lock-step with a real human conversation.

I work on these models in Guanhua Chen's group. My current focus is making spoken-dialogue systems more responsive and natural, with an emphasis on: low-latency speech-text streaming, real-time interruption handling, and tightly coupling audio and text generation inside one unified model.

  • Real-time speech-text interleaving
  • Full-duplex interruption handling
  • Unified multimodal speech + text LLM
  • Streaming inference & low-latency serving

Projects

Muon-nanoGPT

Course project (Mathematical Foundations of AI) on how Newton-Schulz orthogonalization schedules in the Muon optimizer affect GPT pretraining. Muon variants reach a final validation loss of 4.28–4.53 vs 5.37 for AdamW; multi-seed runs confirm fast coefficients (fast5) as the strongest practical baseline, while deeper orthogonalization improves update geometry without further loss gains.

Report
Muon Optimization GPT Pretraining

LongContextLM Survey

NLP course project asking what determines long-context ability in LLMs: a survey with experimental analysis that decomposes long-context ability into nominal window, effective retrieval length, long-range reasoning, and system efficiency, benchmarking a dense Transformer (Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct) against an SSM (Falcon3-Mamba-7B-Instruct) on RULER and LongBench, plus TTFT/TPOT/memory profiling of HF vs vLLM serving.
